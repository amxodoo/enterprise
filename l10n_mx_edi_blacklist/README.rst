Block CFDI stamping of customer invoices and credit notes when the
customer RFC is on the SAT blacklist of article 69-B of the CFF
(Presunto or Definitivo).

The check runs before the invoice is sent to the PAC. When the RFC is
listed, Odoo stops stamping with an error dialog and posts the same text
on the invoice chatter, including the official SAT reference (número y
fecha de oficio). Taxpayers published as Desvirtuado or Sentencia
Favorable are not blocked.

Installation
============

This module depends on Mexican EDI (``l10n_mx_edi``), which is part of
Odoo Enterprise, and on ``l10n_mx_sat_69b`` from OCA/l10n-mexico for the
SAT article 69-B list.

Configuration
=============

No configuration is required.

The SAT list is downloaded by ``l10n_mx_sat_69b``. Generic RFCs
``XAXX010101000`` and ``XEXX010101000``, and invoices sent to the
general public, are not checked.

Usage
=====

1. Install **Mexican EDI - SAT Blacklist** on a company that already
   uses Mexican EDI.
2. Confirm a customer invoice (or credit note) whose partner RFC is a
   real taxpayer RFC.
3. Send the CFDI the usual way (Send & Print, or the CFDI retry action).
4. When the RFC is **Presunto** or **Definitivo** on SAT list 69-B:

   - the CFDI is not stamped;
   - a dialog shows the customer, the RFC, the SAT status, and the
     official reference (oficio);
   - the same text stays on the invoice chatter.

5. When the RFC is not on the list, or is **Desvirtuado** or **Sentencia
   Favorable**, stamping continues as usual.
6. When the SAT list cannot be downloaded and there is no cached copy,
   the invoice is not stamped and the chatter explains that the
   blacklist could not be verified.
